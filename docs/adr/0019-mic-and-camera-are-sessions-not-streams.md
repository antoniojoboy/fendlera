# ADR-0019 - Mic and camera are sessions, not streams

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-09-08

**Context**
An always-open sensor is a sensor you have to trust.

**Decision**
The microphone follows the device in use and the carrier she came through. The camera is explicit open and close.

**Consequences**
Reinforced by 0033: ambient transcription is out of scope.
