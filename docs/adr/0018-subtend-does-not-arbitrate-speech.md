# ADR-0018 - Subtend does not arbitrate speech

**Status:** Accepted. Clarified 13 Sep: input only

**Date:** 2026-09-08

**Context**
Language needs the model; symbols do not.

**Decision**
Speech never resolves through Subtend. She satisfies open claims from speech herself.

**Consequences**
"Resolve" means arbitrate, not carry. Outbound speech does route through Subtend (0036). Speech, text, video and camera streams go to Fendlera; Subtend is only ever the periphery.
