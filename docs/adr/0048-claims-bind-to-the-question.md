# ADR-0048 - Claims bind to the question

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-09-13

**Context**
A carrier can vanish mid-question.

**Decision**
A claim is a bid for an answer, not a button. Losing a carrier loses a route, not the claim; the question falls back to speech.

**Consequences**
She phrases questions from what is available: "yes or no" aloud when no press exists.
